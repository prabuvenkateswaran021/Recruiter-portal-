🔎 Follow-up & Action Required

This report gives a consolidated view of the interview pipeline — scheduled interviews, completed interviews, pending feedback, and other statuses — for the selected month or day. Request all recruiters to review their respective interview schedules and ensure that:
  • All scheduled interviews are closely monitored.
  • Candidate availability is reconfirmed before the interview.
  • Pending interview statuses are updated promptly.
  • Candidate and client feedback is followed up and updated.
  • Any rescheduled or cancelled interviews are reflected correctly.
  • Final outcomes are updated wherever the interview has been completed.
  • Any pending interviews or feedback are followed up and closed on priority.

Kindly review the overall, recruiter-wise, and client-wise interview status counts for both the month and day views below, and take the necessary follow-up actions to ensure timely updates and closures.
